Moving out for the summer? We make it easy:

We provide all packing supplies—boxes, tape, labels, and bubble wrap.
You choose a pickup day, and we collect your packed items from your dorm.
We store them safely for the summer.
When you return in the fall, your belongings will be waiting in your room.

Need an extra hand moving out? We’re here to help:

We pick up your packed items and help you move out of your dorm or apartment.
We help you move to another location or to your car.
Flexible scheduling to fit your move-out timeline.

Fraternities, sororities, and themed housing get special group rates on moving services:

Coordinated pickups & deliveries for houses and members.
Discounted pricing for bulk moves.
Effortless transitions to make moving easier for everyone.

Why Dorm to Door?

Dorm to Door Our Competitors
Service

Who Knows Students Better Than Students?

As classmates, we know campus better than anyone—from the toughest dorms to access to the best times to move. We work around finals schedules to make your move as smooth and stress-free as possible. With us, you’re getting a service designed by students who truly understand your needs.

They Don’t Know Your Campus Like We Do

Competitors lack a student presence, making moving and storage inconvenient and disconnected from campus life.They can’t offer the flexibility and ease that students need.

Support

Students Helping Students

Need help? Our team is made up of students you know and trust, ready to assist with pickups, deliveries, and any questions—right on campus when you need us.

No On-Campus Support? No Thanks.

Many storage services aren’t on campus, making it hard to get quick answers or resolve issues in person. Slow response times and lack of face-to-face assistance only add to the stress

Cost

Same Price, Regardless Of What You Store

No hidden fees, no surprises—just one flat rate for all your items. With us, you'll know exactly what you're paying upfront for a stress-free storage experience.


Group Discounts Available

(Best Price Around)

Moving out with roommates or friends? Store your belongings together and save more for each person added. The bigger the group, the bigger the discount—because moving is easier (and cheaper) together!

Hidden Fees Add Up

Most storage services charge by the box, per month, causing your costs to skyrocket fast. What seems affordable at first can quickly add up, especially with extra charges for larger items like fridges, lamps, mattresses, and bed frames.
